## Communication Requirements

**Communication Cadence**: No formal reporting required [Q7]. The solo developer operates independently with full autonomy.

**Artifacts & Deliverables**: 
- Game executable/playable build
- Design and requirements documentation (for learning purposes)
- Source code with clear structure and comments

## Decision Authority

**Scope & Priority**: The solo developer retains complete decision authority [Q6]. Scope decisions are made autonomously without external approval gates.

**Design Choices**: The developer decides all architectural, UI, and gameplay design decisions.

**Timeline**: The developer controls the timeline and prioritization of work.
